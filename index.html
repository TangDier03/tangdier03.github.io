01 — About

About

I am a second-year PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at The University of Hong Kong (expected 2029), advised by Professor Guangyue Han. I obtained my BSc degree in Mathematics from Jilin University, TAQ Honors Program in Science, in 2025.

My current research and primary research interests include:

  • Information Theory for Learning and Inference
  • High-Dimensional Feature Selection and Representation Learning
  • Optimization in Deep Learning and Reinforcement Learning
  • Foundational Frameworks for AI-driven Scientific Discovery

I am always open to exploring new topics and long-term collaborations. Feel free to reach out if you share similar research interests or would like to discuss potential ideas!

02 — News

News

(2026.09-)
  • 2026-10-05 On the Cardinality of Optimal Representations in the Binary-Source Information Bottleneck was uploaded to arXiv. We proved Chen's conjecture that, for the information bottleneck with a binary source, a binary representation is always optimal, so the tight cardinality bound is exactly 2 rather than the classical bound of 3. ↗
  • 2026-09-29 Mutual Information Constrained Chernoff Bottleneck was uploaded to arXiv. We proposed a generalized information bottleneck that replaces the relevance term with the Chernoff error exponent of the downstream binary test, established its curve shape and cardinality bound, and developed an alternating algorithm combining a generalized Blahut-Arimoto algorithm and a nonlinear equation solver. ↗
  • 2026-09-14 Sharp Regret Bounds and a Task-Covariance Correction for Spectral Representation Learning was uploaded to arXiv. We show that spectral features transfer by aligning with the directions tasks use, not by isotropy, and that only the task covariance matters. A spectral gap gives a criterion for when regret is bounded quadratically rather than linearly in anisotropy, and a task-covariance correction fixes poorly aligned features even in a frozen encoder without retraining. ↗
